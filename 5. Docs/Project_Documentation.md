# Mzansi Retail Sales & Customer Churn Dashboard

## Project Documentation

### 1. Introduction

This project analyses retail sales performance and customer metrics for a fictional South African retailer, Mzansi Retail.

The project uses MySQL for querying and analysing data and Microsoft Power BI for visualising results in an interactive dashboard.

The objective is to demonstrate how data analytics and business intelligence tools can be used to investigate business performance and communicate findings to decision-makers.

### 2. Business Problem

Retail organisations need timely information about revenue, order volumes, regional performance, product category contributions, and customer retention.

Without structured analysis and reporting, identifying performance patterns and areas requiring management attention can be difficult.

This project brings these metrics together to support business performance monitoring.

### 3. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Calculate key sales performance indicators.
- Analyse revenue across geographical regions.
- Compare performance across product categories.
- Examine customer retention and churn indicators.
- Present findings through a Power BI dashboard.
- Develop recommendations based on the recorded results.

### 4. Tools and Technologies

**MySQL:** Used to store and query relational data.

**SQL:** Used for filtering, joining, aggregating, and analysing data.

**Microsoft Power BI:** Used to create interactive visualisations and present business performance.

**DAX:** Used to calculate and display key performance indicators.

### 5. Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured analytical workflow.

1. Organise and query the retail data.
2. Use SQL to aggregate and analyse sales performance.
3. Calculate relevant business metrics.
4. Load the required data into Power BI.
5. Develop DAX measures for reporting.
6. Create KPI cards and charts.
7. Review the dashboard and interpret the results.
8. Develop recommendations based on the analysis.

### 6. Key Findings

#### Overall Performance

The dashboard recorded total revenue of R129,124.60 across 30 orders.

The average order value implied by these figures is approximately R4,304.15.

#### Regional Performance

Gauteng recorded 15 orders and revenue of R65,844.83, making it the highest-revenue region in the reported results.

Free State recorded revenue of R24,704.89, KwaZulu-Natal recorded R20,299.91, and Western Cape recorded R18,274.97.

#### Product Category Performance

Electronics recorded revenue of R98,624.86 across 13 orders.

Clothing recorded revenue of R16,409.87 across 9 orders, while Home & Garden recorded revenue of R14,089.87 across 8 orders.

Electronics was the largest revenue-generating category in the reported results.

#### Customer Retention

The dashboard recorded a retention rate of 40% and a churn rate of 60%.

These figures require interpretation in the context of the dataset's customer definitions and observation period.

### 7. Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the factors behind Gauteng's revenue performance.
2. Assess the business's reliance on Electronics revenue.
3. Investigate repeat purchasing behaviour and customer retention.
4. Review sales performance regularly to identify changes and emerging trends.
5. Expand the historical customer dataset to support stronger churn analysis.

### 8. Limitations

The findings reflect the data available for this project.

Customer churn and retention calculations are sensitive to how customers are classified and the period used for analysis. The current results should not be treated as proof of long-term customer behaviour without appropriate supporting data.

### 9. Future Improvements

Potential extensions include:

- Adding more historical sales data.
- Analysing customer purchasing frequency.
- Developing customer segmentation.
- Building a predictive churn model.
- Automating data ingestion and transformation.
- Integrating additional data sources.

### 10. Conclusion

The project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be combined to analyse retail performance and communicate business insights.

It provides practical experience in data querying, KPI development, data visualisation, and translating analytical results into business recommendations.
